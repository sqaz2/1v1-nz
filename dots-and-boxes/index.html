How to play

  1. Tap between two dots to draw a line.
  2. Close a box (draw its 4th side) to claim it — it fills with your color.
  3. Every box you close earns another turn. Keep chaining as long as you keep closing boxes!
  4. When the board is full, most boxes wins.

Strategy: don't just grab single boxes — set up chains where one line closes multiple boxes at once. That's how you sweep the board.
