FAQ

What is 1v1.nz?

1v1.nz is a collection of free 1 versus 1 browser games — Battleship and Connect 4 plus StarMuff 1v1 clones (Tetris, Pinball, Simon, Skirmish).

Can I play vs a friend?

Battleship supports online 1v1. Tetris, Pinball, Simon, and Skirmish support hotseat (same device) and vs CPU today; online friend matches are coming soon.

Do I need an account?

No. Every game on 1v1.nz runs free in the browser with no sign-up required.
