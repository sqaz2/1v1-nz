FAQ

What is 1v1.nz?

1v1.nz is a collection of free 1 versus 1 multiplayer browser games — classic head-to-head titles like Battleship and Connect 4, playable on phone or desktop.

Yes. Battleship supports online 1v1 matches against a friend. Connect 4 currently plays vs the computer, with friend modes planned.

Do I need an account?

No. Every game on 1v1.nz runs free in the browser with no sign-up required.
